Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

This policy explains how data is handled when you use PeakCut. PeakCut is self-hosted software: the operator of the particular PeakCut installation controls its server, storage, settings and access. If you run PeakCut yourself, you are the operator. This policy describes the standard software behavior; an operator may provide additional notices for their installation.

1. Data you provide or process

Depending on features you use, PeakCut may process video files or source links, audio, transcripts, clip selections, captions, titles, creator settings and account connection data. Social sign-in or posting features may return account identifiers, profile details, access tokens and posting status from the platform you connect.

2. Where data goes

  • Video, transcripts, clips, settings and connected-account data are stored by the PeakCut instance operator on the computer or server where that instance runs.
  • Speech transcription is performed locally by the standard installation. If you configure an AI provider, text from your video or transcript may be sent to that provider to generate clip selections, hooks, titles or captions. The provider's own privacy terms apply.
  • If you connect a social account, PeakCut sends the information needed to request authorization and the actions you choose to that platform. The platform's privacy policy applies to its handling of the data.
  • This GitHub Pages website is hosted by GitHub. GitHub may process standard request and security data when you visit it under GitHub's own policies.

3. How data is used

Data is used to run PeakCut features you request, keep your settings, connect accounts, prepare or publish clips, show job status, and protect the installation. PeakCut does not use forex content to place trades or provide investment recommendations.

4. Retention and deletion

The installation operator controls storage and retention. Generated clips, uploads, account data, logs and backups may remain until deleted by the operator or user, depending on the installation. Disconnecting an account may stop future access but may not erase data already stored by the social platform. Contact the installation operator to ask about deletion.

5. Security

Self-hosted installations depend on the operator's computer, network, configuration, backups and security practices. Do not expose a local or personal installation to the public internet without appropriate protections. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Children and choices

PeakCut is not intended for children under 13. You can choose whether to connect social accounts or an AI provider. You can also choose which videos to process and whether to publish generated clips.

7. Your privacy requests

For a self-hosted instance, contact the person who operates that instance about its data. For this project website, use GitHub Issues to request a private contact method. Do not place personal data, account identifiers, tokens, passwords or private video links in a public issue. Rights and choices available to you depend on your location and applicable law.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated as the software changes. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.

Important: This site is for information about PeakCut. PeakCut is not a forex broker, adviser, signal provider or trading service.
